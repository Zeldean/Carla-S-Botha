## School and ATKV speeches

Every speech is written for the learner's age and grade. You receive:

- a typed speech
- a voice note showing how the speech can be presented

Optional extras: visual learning aids for younger learners, presentation preparation and PowerPoint presentations.

## How ordering works

1. Contact Carla about the speech.
2. The speech is prepared.
3. Carla sends a voice note and an invoice.
4. After proof of payment, the typed speech is sent as a PDF.
5. Print the speech and prepare cue cards.
